# References and Further Reading 📚

## Academic Sources

1. **Shockley, W. & Queisser, H.J.** (1961). *Detailed Balance Limit of Efficiency of p-n Junction Solar Cells*. Journal of Applied Physics, 32(3), 510–519. — The foundational paper establishing the 33.7% theoretical limit.

2. **Einstein, A.** (1905). *On a Heuristic Point of View Concerning the Production and Transformation of Light*. Annalen der Physik, 17, 132–148. — The Nobel Prize-winning photoelectric effect paper.

3. **Planck, M.** (1901). *On the Law of Distribution of Energy in the Normal Spectrum*. Annalen der Physik, 4, 553–563. — Birth of quantum theory.

4. **Betz, A.** (1926). *Wind-Energie und ihre Ausnutzung durch Windmühlen*. Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, Göttingen. — Original derivation of the 59.3% Betz limit.

## Institutional Reports

5. **International Energy Agency (IEA)** — *World Energy Outlook 2024*. Paris: IEA. Available at: https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-outlook-2024

6. **International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA)** — *Renewable Power Generation Costs in 2023*. Abu Dhabi: IRENA. Available at: https://www.irena.org/Publications

7. **National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL)** — *Best Research-Cell Efficiency Chart*. Updated continuously. Available at: https://www.nrel.gov/pv/cell-efficiency.html

8. **European Space Agency (ESA)** — *SOLARIS: Space-Based Solar Power Initiative*, 2022. Available at: https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Engineering_Technology/SOLARIS

## Textbooks for Deeper Study

9. **Green, M.A.** (1982). *Solar Cells: Operating Principles, Technology and System Applications*. Prentice-Hall. — The classic solar cell textbook.

10. **Nelson, J.** (2003). *The Physics of Solar Cells*. Imperial College Press, London. — Comprehensive coverage of PV physics.

11. **Würfel, P. & Würfel, U.** (2016). *Physics of Solar Cells: From Basic Principles to Advanced Concepts* (3rd ed.). Wiley-VCH. — Advanced but readable treatment of cell physics.

12. **Kittel, C.** (2004). *Introduction to Solid State Physics* (8th ed.). Wiley. — Standard reference for band theory and semiconductor physics.

## Online Resources and Databases

13. **Our World in Data — Energy** — https://ourworldindata.org/energy  
    Interactive charts tracking global energy production, solar costs, and CO₂ data with full source citations.

14. **PVWatts Calculator (NREL)** — https://pvwatts.nrel.gov/  
    Official tool for estimating solar energy output at any location worldwide, including Bosnia.

15. **Open Energy Information (OpenEI)** — https://openei.org/  
    US Department of Energy open database of energy data, maps, and tools.

16. **Fraunhofer ISE — Current Facts on Photovoltaics in Germany** — https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/  
    Annual report on solar integration in a high-renewable grid — directly applicable to European context.
